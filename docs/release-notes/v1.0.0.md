The first release of LoopTexturer, a VST® 3 insert plug-in for sound design: an effects chain feeding a tape-style looper, whose audio you can drag straight onto the timeline.

## What's in it

- **Effects chain:** Gain, Pan and a multimode Filter, each of the last two with its own LFO, a saturator with six modes, and Volume.
- **Looper, free running:** works like an endless tape loop, whether or not your DAW is playing. Length runs from 500 ms to 10 s, and changing it repitches the loop.
- **Looper, tempo sync:** follows the transport, with recording and playback starting on the next bar line. Loops can be up to 64 bars, and a tempo change repitches them.
- **Overdub and feedback:** after its first pass, Rec moves on to overdubbing. Feedback goes up to 200%, for pushing loops into distortion.
- **Post saturator and dry/wet:** a second saturator colours the loop's output without changing what's recorded.
- **Drag to timeline:** drag the waveform into your DAW to get the loop as you hear it, as a WAV file.
- **Saved with your project:** the loop is stored in the plug-in's state.

## Installing

Download `LoopTexturer-1.0.0-Setup.exe` below and run it. It installs to `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`, which is where DAWs look by default, and you can remove it from Windows' Installed apps.

**Windows will probably warn you when you run it,** because the installer isn't code-signed. To continue, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or later, 64-bit
- A VST 3 host. LoopTexturer is developed and used in Cubase, and other hosts haven't been tried. Dragging to the timeline depends on the host accepting file drops from a plug-in window.
